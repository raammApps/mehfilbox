## Signing in

**Where:** `/login` — a Studio tab and a **Client** tab on the same page. Pick Client.

You get an account one of two ways:

- **Your studio hands you one** — see below. They set it up from their side; you set your own
  password the first time you sign in.
- **You start a catalogue of your own** — see [Starting your own](#starting-your-own). This
  creates your account for you.

Forgot your password: **"Reset it"** on the sign-in form sends a single-use link.

## Your weddings

**Where:** `/my`

Everything made for you, from **any studio**, in one place — one card per catalogue, each showing
its status and who made it. A studio linking a wedding to your email address is enough for it to
appear here; nothing else to do.

---

## What you can do once it is yours

A wedding a studio has **handed over** to you gives your console the same tools the studio had,
minus the ability to hand it to someone else:

- add and remove films and photographs,
- rearrange and rename sections, change branding, change the theme,
- publish and unpublish,
- change privacy and the passcode,
- delete the catalogue.

You cannot see any other wedding on the platform, including ones another studio built for someone
else, and your studio cannot see yours once it is handed over — unless you open a **support
window** for them (see [Handing access back to your studio](#handing-access-back-to-your-studio)).

A wedding **not yet handed over** — one your studio is still building, or one you started yourself
before it is published — shows here too, so you can watch it come together, but editing happens on
the studio's side until it is handed to you (or, for one you started, from the moment you create
it).

> **Renewal and buying storage are not built yet.** Today, extending how long a wedding keeps
> serving guests is something the studio or the platform does by hand. There is no payment flow
> yet.

## Downloading

**Where:** a catalogue → `/download` on its guest link, or from your own `/my` console.

**A passcode lets a guest *watch*. It does not let anyone download.** Downloading — including
original, full-resolution files — needs a sign-in: yours, if the wedding is yours, or your
studio's while they still hold it. If you open a passcode-protected wedding's download page
without being signed in, you are shown a sign-in prompt instead of a file list.

This is deliberate: a code shared at the wedding for guests to watch should not also be a way to
carry off every original file forever.

## Starting your own

**Where:** `/my` → **Start a catalogue of your own**

The same five-step wizard a studio uses, in its own shape for you: the occasion comes first
(wedding, engagement, anniversary, birthday, proposal, baby shower, naming day), there are no
house styles to choose from since those belong to studios, and there is no step for "the couple's
sign-in" — it is already you.

A catalogue you start this way is a **draft until a credit is added**, by us or by a studio you
ask — the same ₹1,999 a studio's second-and-later wedding costs. Nothing about the page, the films
or the draft is lost while you wait; publishing works the moment a credit lands.

## Handing access back to your studio

**Where:** your console → the catalogue → the panel that opens a **support window**.

If you want your studio to fix or re-style something after they have already handed the wedding to
you, open a window for them — **seven or fourteen days**, your choice. It closes on its own; there
is nothing to remember to switch off, and nothing they do while it is open is hidden from you.

## What your guests see

No account, ever. Whoever opens your link gets:

1. **The profile gate** — *"Who's joining?"* with four fixed choices: Bride's side, Groom's side,
   Friends, Family. **Never a free-text name.** They can skip it.
2. The catalogue itself — billboard, rows, whatever was arranged.
3. A film opens in a modal, then plays on its own page.

Worth knowing:

- **Resume works.** Position is remembered; "Start over" abandons it rather than arguing.
- **Sharing** uses the phone's own share sheet (WhatsApp, on a phone) with a copy-link fallback.
  A film's link can carry `?t=` so it opens at a specific moment — *"watch from 7:08"*.
  A photograph's link opens that exact photograph.
- **Likes** on films and photographs are counted across every guest and shown to all of them, not
  kept private — per device, not per person, so signing in is never required to tap one.
- English and Hindi, switchable by the guest at any time regardless of what was set when the
  wedding was created.
- If a **premiere** date was set, guests see a countdown until it rather than the wedding itself.
- Past the *serving until* date, guests see a renewal screen — never a broken link — before
  anything else.

## Limits, and what is not built yet

**Storage is the only limit**, set by whichever plan your wedding is on, and a catalogue can be
granted more than the default without waiting on your studio if they are no longer involved.

Not built yet, as of this writing:

- **Paying for renewal, archive or extra storage yourself.** Today this is a studio or platform
  action, done by hand.
- **A notification telling you the wedding is now yours.** You learn this from your studio, not
  from us — ask them if you are not sure whether a handover has happened.
- **An in-app way to change your email or see your account details** beyond password reset.

## When something goes wrong

| Symptom | Cause |
|---|---|
| **A wedding you expect is not on `/my`** | Your studio has not yet linked it to this email address, or has not yet handed it over — ask them. |
| **You cannot download something** | You need to be signed in — a passcode alone only lets guests watch. |
| **A handover link says "no longer valid"** | It was already used, has expired, or was cancelled. Ask your studio for a new one. |
| **Signing in on your studio's device shows their weddings, not yours** | Signing in on the handover confirmation screen ends whatever session is already on that device first — sign in again if that did not happen. |
| **Your studio can suddenly edit your wedding again** | A support window is open — check the panel where you opened it, or close it early. |
